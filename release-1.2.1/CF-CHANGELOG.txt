1.2.1 - addon pack trains can now be filtered too

1.2.0 let you pick which BUILT-IN MTR trains to keep. This update extends the same picker to trains added by ADDON PACKS, so a 100-car metro pack no longer floods your train menu. Nothing about the 1.2.0 flow changed - if you never open the addon tab, the mod behaves exactly as before.


=== NEW: ADDON PACK TRAIN FILTER ===

- The main menu button now opens a small hub with three choices: Built-in trains, Addon pack trains, and Cancel. Built-in trains leads to the screen you already know.
- Addon packs are listed by name with a car count next to each one. Click a pack to drill in. Back returns to the pack list, Done saves.
- Only real train packs show up. A pack is listed only if it actually ships an mtr:mtr_custom_resources.json declaring vehicles. Texture packs, shader packs, sound packs and anything else sitting in your resource pack folder are never scanned in.
- Both pack formats are supported. Modern packs (vehicles[]) and old-style packs (custom_trains) are read the same way, so a pack that has not been updated for MTR 4 still lists its trains properly.
- Trains are grouped by family, derived from the names and IDs the pack itself declares. Most packs carry no family tag at all - a pack with 109 cars still collapses into 16 readable rows instead of 109 loose entries. Click + to expand a family and tick individual cars, or tick the family to block all of them at once.
- Names come from the pack manifest, not from the running game, so a train keeps its real name even after you have blocked it. The row does not degrade into a raw ID like mtr_custom_train_ac01_cab_1 the next time you open the screen.
- Per-pack and overall counters. Each pack row shows how many of its cars you have blocked, and the hub keeps a running total across all packs.


=== BEHAVIOUR ===

- Addon trains are never touched unless you explicitly tick them. By default every addon pack car is allowed through, identical to 1.2.0.
- Built-in keep rules always win. If a built-in train is on your keep list, no addon setting can remove it.
- Saving goes through the same restart dialog as the built-in picker: restart now, restart later, or never ask again. Nothing takes effect until the game restarts.
- Trains only, never boats or aircraft. The picker counts and filters vehicles whose transport mode is TRAIN; pack-declared boats, airships and cable cars are left alone.


=== FIXED ===

- The main menu button showed raw translation keys on Forge (gui.mtr_no_original_trains.tab_builtin and friends) instead of text, because Forge keeps its own copy of the language files and the new keys had only been added on the Fabric side. Both sides are now generated from the same source.


=== FILE NAMES ===

Jars keep the loader in the file name: mtr_no_original_trains-fabric-1.2.1.jar and mtr_no_original_trains-forge-1.2.1.jar. Delete the old jar before installing; both builds share the same mod ID and will conflict if you keep two.

Requirements: Minecraft 1.20.1, Fabric Loader 0.14.21+ or Forge 47.x, Minecraft Transit Railway 4.0.0 or newer, plus FerriteCore and ModernFix (both are hard requirements since 1.1.0).
